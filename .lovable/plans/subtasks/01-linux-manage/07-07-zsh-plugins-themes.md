## Where
- `01-ubuntu-installation.md`, `02-fedora-installation.md`, `03-centos-installation.md`
## What
Translate: `ZSH_THEME="agnoster"`, `plugins=(git zsh-autosuggestions)`.
## How
- **All**: Detail editing `~/.zshrc`.
- Add `git clone https://github.com/zsh-users/zsh-autosuggestions ${ZSH_CUSTOM:-~/.oh-my-zsh/custom}/plugins/zsh-autosuggestions`.
- Add instructions to change `ZSH_THEME` to `agnoster` or `amuse`.
