Why is the UI broken or unrefined? Check responsive layout (no overflow, clipped controls, or collapsed panels), padding and spacing rhythm, font contrast on every background including hover and disabled states, text sizes and truncation, visible hover, focus, and active states on every interactive element, and alignment of controls, icons, and labels — then verify in the real interface and report what changed, file by file.
