## Time & Effort Mandate

Spend adequate time on this work. Depth comes before speed: do not skim
the task, do not summarize and stop, and do not declare anything done
from a partial reading. Work through every item in the batch, one at a
time, and verify each one against its acceptance criteria before moving
to the next. If a requirement is unclear, investigate further in the
codebase (GitMap search, file inspection) rather than guessing or
assuming it is satisfied. A short answer is not a finished task; a
finished task is one where every item has been implemented, checked, and
evidenced.

Thoroughness here means thoroughness within the wave: planning, triage,
and implementation depth. It does NOT lift the wave rule — do not build
the code and do not run end-to-end tests until the terminal verification
gate is reached (see the Terminal Verification Gate section of the
dispatched prompt). Spend the time where it counts: understanding the
existing code, making surgical changes, and recording evidence.
